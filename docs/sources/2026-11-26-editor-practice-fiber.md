[IUSD, ответы 1.26 и 1.41](https://iusd.org/sites/default/files/documents/rfi_no._i_1.pdf) · [BC Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure, §305.2, с. 24](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/driving-and-transportation/transportation-infrastructure/engineering-standards-and-guidelines/electrical/fibre-reference-guide.pdf).
